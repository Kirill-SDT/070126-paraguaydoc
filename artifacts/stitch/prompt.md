# Stitch Design Prompt

## Project: Paraguaydoc Immigration Services Landing Page

## CRITICAL: Output Requirements
**IMPORTANT:** Generate 3 DISTINCT design variations (Version A, Version B, Version C).
**LANGUAGE:** All content MUST be in RUSSIAN (ru) - this is a Russian-language immigration service website.
**CONTENT SOURCE:** Use ONLY content from this prompt - DO NOT invent features, pages, or menu items not specified below.

## Target Audience & Context

**Target Audience:**
- **Primary Segment:** Russian-speaking families or remote professionals aged 30-50 seeking simple and transparent temporary residency in Paraguay. Budget: $5,000-10,000. Main fears: bureaucratic delays lasting years, losing money to scams. Need clear, step-by-step process.
- **Secondary Segment:** Business owners with capital $70k+ seeking permanent residency and business opportunities in Paraguay. Need speed, efficiency, and investment security.
- **Demographics:** Age 30-50, from Russia/CIS countries, already decided to relocate and researching service providers.

**Primary Goal:** Application form submission, followed by Telegram consultation

**Value Proposition:** "Прозрачная иммиграция в Парагвай без бюрократии и скрытых платежей — проводим вас за руку от заявки до Cédula"

## Brand Identity

**Emotions to express:**
- Trust (надежность)
- Transparency (прозрачность)
- Calm confidence (спокойная уверенность)
- Human connection (человечность)
- Reliability (забота)

**Brand Personality:** Experienced lawyer-friend who has guided 100+ families through the immigration process and knows all pitfalls. NOT a cold corporate office, NOT an aggressive salesperson. Like having a good cup of coffee - gives a sense of control.

**Competitive Advantage:** Transparent pricing, real people, step-by-step guidance, no aggressive sales tactics, located in Encarnación (not Asunción). Shows real process, real people, concrete timelines and prices.

**Color Palette:**
- Primary: #2563eb (blue - trust, reliability)
- Secondary: #10b981 (green - growth, success)
- Accent: #f59e0b (amber - warmth, attention)
- Neutral Dark: #1f2937 (text)
- Neutral Light: #f9fafb (backgrounds)
- Error: #ef4444 (red)
- Success: #10b981 (green)

**Typography Style:**
- Headings: Inter or similar modern sans-serif
- Body: Inter or similar readable sans-serif
- Font sizes: H1: 36-48px (mobile: 28-32px), H2: 30-36px (mobile: 24-28px), Body: 16-18px

**Design Philosophy:** Prioritize clarity and trust over flashiness. Better to be honest and straightforward than to oversell. The site should feel like a reliable friend explaining a complex process, not a pushy salesperson.

## Key Success Criteria

1. **Trust & Transparency:** Design should feel honest, open, professional - like talking to a knowledgeable friend
2. **Clear Pricing:** All prices, timelines, and process steps must be immediately visible and scannable
3. **Telegram Focus:** Telegram as the primary conversion channel should be prominent throughout
4. **Mobile Experience:** Most users will be on mobile - prioritize mobile layout and usability
5. **No Aggressive Tactics:** No countdown timers, no fake urgency, no "limited spots" pressure
6. **Russian Language:** All text in Russian - this is critical for the target audience
7. **Process Clarity:** Users should understand exactly what happens, when, and how much it costs

## Final Notes

This is a landing page for a real immigration services business in Paraguay serving Russian-speaking clients. The brand values transparency and human connection over flashy marketing. The goal is to generate qualified leads who understand the process and pricing before they contact via Telegram.

Focus on clarity, trust-building, and making the complex immigration process feel manageable and transparent. The design should reduce anxiety and build confidence, not create urgency or pressure.

All 3 design variations should maintain these core principles while exploring different visual approaches to the same content and goals.
